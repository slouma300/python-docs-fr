Vercel-ready inventory

Stock by location

Receive stock, remove stock, transfer between locations, and scan barcodes. This version is ready for Vercel and prepared for a later Supabase database connection.

Storage mode

Right now this Vercel app saves data in this browser only. That is good for demos. When Supabase is connected later, inventory will be saved in the cloud and shared across devices.

Barcode scanner

Click the barcode field and scan with a USB/Bluetooth scanner. If your browser supports the Barcode Detection API, you can also use the camera scanner below.
